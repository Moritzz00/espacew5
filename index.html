Project description

COEXIST is an artistic project taking place on the premises of an old residential building in Luxembourg City. The project combines a residency programme for artists from various disciplines and backgrounds with a related exhibition. The programme also includes a series of workshops that relate to the exhibition in terms of theme and content, as well as a small art and flea market.\n\nThe format is designed to invite the public to immerse themselves in a creative world and explore the theme of coexistence together with the artists. We welcome everyone to come and coexist with us :)
